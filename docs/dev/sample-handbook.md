# Sample Handbook (made up, for local development)

This file is invented test content, not Ingage policy. Ingest it locally
(`HANDBOOK_LOCAL_PATH=docs/dev/sample-handbook.md pnpm ingest --source handbook`) to try answers
and citations before the real handbook is available.

## Time Off

### Paid Time Off

Employees request paid time off in the HR system at least two weeks before the first day off.
Your coach approves the request.

### Holidays

The company observes ten paid holidays a year. The list for the coming year is published every
December.

## Learning

### Training Budget

Each employee has an annual training budget for courses, certifications, and conferences.
Spend from it needs your coach's approval before you book.

## Performance

### Reviews

Performance reviews happen twice a year, in June and December. Your coach collects feedback
from the people you worked with before each review.

## Growth Pillars

Non-client time is logged in QuickBooks under one of three pillars. Client work is Client
Impact and is billed to the client, not logged here.

### Personal Growth

Time spent building your own skills: courses, certifications, study groups, conferences, and
lunch-and-learn sessions.

### Ingage Growth

Time spent making Ingage better: recruiting, onboarding new hires, internal committees and
growth teams, and practice-area work.

### Community Impact

Time spent volunteering with community organizations, on your own or at Ingage-organized
service events.
